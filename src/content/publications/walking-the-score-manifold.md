We frame generative modeling of time-dependent data as continuous-time evolution on a learned data manifold: pretrained score-based models serve as geometric priors, and a vector field is trained simulation-free to evolve data along score-induced interpolation paths. This enables generation at arbitrary timestamps and temporal super-resolution beyond the training discretization, with a stability objective for robust long-horizon rollouts and a probabilistic extension over plausible future trajectories, demonstrated on natural video, PDE-based spatiotemporal fields, and molecular dynamics.
