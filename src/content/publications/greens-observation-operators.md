We introduce the Green's Observation Operator (GObO), which maps the ambient medium once to the Green's kernel of a linear PDE restricted to the source and observation submanifolds, so that each new source costs only a lower-dimensional integral and no network evaluation. On 3D heat conduction and advection–diffusion, GObO trained on static sources predicts responses to moving sources zero-shot with 4–8× lower error than black-box surrogates, admits an exact finite streaming state with horizon-independent memory, and transfers across resolutions and mild nonlinearities without retraining.
